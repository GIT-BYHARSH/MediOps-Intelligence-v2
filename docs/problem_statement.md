# MediOps Intelligence: AI-Powered Healthcare Revenue Recovery Platform

## Problem Statement
Multi-hospital networks lose time and potentially recoverable revenue because insurance claims are denied, partially reimbursed, or left unpaid for extended periods. Finance teams often lack a unified, data-driven way to identify the causes of these issues, estimate recoverable amounts, and prioritize follow-up actions.

MediOps Intelligence transforms healthcare billing and insurance-claim data into actionable recovery intelligence. The platform identifies high-priority claims, analyzes recurring denial patterns, estimates potential cash recovery, and provides an interactive risk dashboard, an AI-assisted analytical copilot, and a daily recovery brief.

## Primary Objective
Help a multi-hospital finance team decide which insurance claims to appeal, correct or follow up on first, by estimating recoverable cash and ranking claims by recovery value and effort.

## Success Measures
- Estimated recoverable amount (INR)
- Preventable denial share
- Outstanding-claim aging
- Simulated recovery under different follow-up strategies

## Note on Data
All data in this project is simulated. Patterns are deliberately planted to test analytical methods.
