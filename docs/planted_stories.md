# Planted Stories

All data is simulated. These patterns are deliberately planted so the analysis has real findings to discover.

1. One insurer rejects far more claims at two specific hospitals (about 28% vs about 10% network-wide), mostly for Incomplete Documents.
2. One complex department at one hospital has a high rate of Coding Error denials.
3. One insurer settles claims much slower (about 60 days vs about 20 for others).
4. In December and January, visits and waiting times rise, and denial rates rise with them.
5. One hospital has a backlog of Pending claims older than 90 days.
6. Claims above 200,000 INR are denied more often, mostly for Medical Necessity Not Met.

## My own story
7. Surgery bills are partially approved far more often than other treatments (insurers cut surgery and room charges), which creates a large Claim Gap even when claims are not fully rejected.
